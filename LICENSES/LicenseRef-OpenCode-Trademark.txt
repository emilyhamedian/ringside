package/contents/icons/opencode.svg is the OpenCode mark as a single-colour
mask, its outline taken from packages/identity/mark.svg in OpenCode's
repository (github.com/anomalyco/opencode), which is MIT-licensed. The OPENCODE
entry in package/contents/ui/code/marks.js holds the same outline as path
data, which Ringside draws in the theme's colours.

OpenCode and the OpenCode mark belong to the OpenCode project. Ringside is not
affiliated with or endorsed by OpenCode and includes the mark only to label its
OpenCode Go item. Including it grants no rights to use the mark.
