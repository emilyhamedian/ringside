package/contents/icons/moonshot.svg is the Moonshot AI logo as a
single-colour mask, after the drawing in LobeHub's icon set
(github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons, MIT). The MOONSHOT entry in
package/contents/ui/code/marks.js holds the same outline as path data, which
Ringside draws in the theme's colours to label its Kimi item.
